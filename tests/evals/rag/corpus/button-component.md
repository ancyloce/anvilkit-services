# Button Component

## Variants

Buttons come in three variants: primary, secondary and ghost. Primary buttons are filled teal, secondary buttons have a slate outline and ghost buttons have no border.

## Shape

Buttons have an 8 pixel corner radius and a minimum height of 40 pixels.

## Focus

Every button shows a 2 pixel focus ring in teal 300 with a 2 pixel offset when it receives keyboard focus.
