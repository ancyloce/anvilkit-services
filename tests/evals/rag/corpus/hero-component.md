# Hero Component

## Props

The Hero component accepts a title, an optional subtitle, a call-to-action label and a call-to-action link. The title is required and limited to 80 characters.

## Layout

On wide screens the Hero places the text on the left and the image on the right. Below 768 pixels the image moves above the text.

## Background

The Hero banner uses a teal gradient background from teal 700 to teal 500. A background image may replace the gradient when it has alternative text.
