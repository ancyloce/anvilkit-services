# Brand Colors

## Primary palette

The primary brand color is teal (#0F766E). Slate (#334155) is used for headings and body text. Sand (#F5F0E6) is the default page background.

## Contrast rules

Text on teal backgrounds must be white and at least 18 pixels bold to keep a contrast ratio of 4.5:1. Never place slate text on teal.

## Dark mode

In dark mode the background becomes slate 900 and teal is lightened to teal 300 so that links keep enough contrast.
